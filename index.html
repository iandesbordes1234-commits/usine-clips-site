Usine Clips

Home · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

Usine Clips is a private publishing tool used by the creators of the TikTok accounts GTA 6 Moments and Math en 1 minute.

It prepares short vertical videos (subtitles, titles, credits to the original creator) and publishes them to the creators' own TikTok accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API, after the account owner has logged in with TikTok and granted permission.

The tool is not offered to the public. It only posts to accounts owned by its operators.
