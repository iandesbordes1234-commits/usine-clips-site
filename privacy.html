Data we access

When an account owner connects TikTok, Usine Clips receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, open ID) through TikTok Login Kit, solely to publish videos to that same account.

How we use it

The token is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to check the status of those uploads. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never published. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). Stored tokens are then deleted.
